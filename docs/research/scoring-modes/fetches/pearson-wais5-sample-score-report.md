## Derived here (arithmetic on the printed numbers, not a quote)

- GAI sum 62 = SI 14 + VC 15 + BD 11 + MR 12 + FW 10 → **GAI = Similarities, Vocabulary, Block Design, Matrix Reasoning, Figure Weights** (no WM, no PS subtest).
- FSIQ sum 82 = 62 + DQ 9 + CD 11 → **FSIQ (7 subtests) = SI, VC, BD, MR, FW, Digit Sequencing, Coding**. One of the seven FSIQ subtests is a processing-speed task.
- CPI sum 42 = DQ 9 + RD 10 + CD 11 + SS 12 → **CPI = the four WM + PS subtests**.
- Each primary index = sum of its two subtests (e.g. PSI 23 = CD 11 + SS 12).
- The PSI carries the widest SEM of the primaries (5.61) and the FSIQ the narrowest (3.00): the 95% band is about ±5 for FSIQ and about ±8–9 for PSI.

## What it does not say

- Reliability coefficients behind those SEMs (those are in the Technical and Interpretive Manual, not public).
- Any timing rule or time bonus.
