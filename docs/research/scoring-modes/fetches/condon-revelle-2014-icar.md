# Condon & Revelle (2014), "The international cognitive ability resource: Development and initial validation of a public-domain measure", Intelligence 43:52–64

> Fetched 2026-10-19 · Peer-reviewed validation paper of ICAR, author PDF. Read for item types, timing, reliability as a function of length (a real-data check on Spearman–Brown), mean proportion correct, and IRT reliability. PDF text extracted with `pdftotext`.

## What it says

- Goals of the first item types: "(2) avoid the use of "timed" items in light of potential technical issues resulting from telemetric assessment … and (3) avoid item content that could be readily referenced elsewhere given the intended use of un-proctored online administrations."
- "None of the items were timed in these administrations as untimed administration was expected to provide more stringent and conservative evaluation of the items' utility when given online (there are no specific reasons precluding timed administrations of the ICAR items, whether online or offline)."
- Item types: "9 Letter and Number Series items, 11 Matrix Reasoning items, 16 Verbal Reasoning items and 24 Three-dimensional Rotation items." Letter and Number Series ask for "the next position in the sequence from among six choices"; Matrix Reasoning stimuli "are 3 × 3 arrays of geometric shapes with one of the nine shapes missing"; "The Verbal Reasoning items include a variety of logic, vocabulary and general knowledge questions."
- Sampling: "Participants were administered 12 to 16 item subsets of the 60 ICAR items using the Synthetic Aperture Personality Assessment ("SAPA") technique"; N = 96,958.
- Table 3, alpha / omega_h / omega_t / items: ICAR60 0.93 / 0.61 / 0.94 / 60 · LN 0.77 / 0.66 / 0.80 / 9 · MR 0.68 / 0.58 / 0.71 / 11 · R3D 0.93 / 0.78 / 0.94 / 24 · VR 0.76 / 0.64 / 0.77 / 16 · ICAR16 0.81 / 0.66 / 0.83 / 16.
- "For the composites based on both 16 and 60 items however, internal consistencies were adequate (α = 0.81; ωtotal = 0.83) and good (α = 0.93; ωtotal = 0.94), respectively. While higher reliabilities reflect the greater number of items in the ICAR60, it should be noted that the general factor saturation was slightly higher for the shorter 16-item measure"
- "the 11 Matrix Reasoning items were not uniformly measuring a singular latent construct"
- IRT: "reliability in this context is calculated as one minus the reciprocal of the test information"; "reliability was generally adequate across a wide range of latent trait levels, and particularly good within approximately ±1.5 standardized units from the mean item difficulty."
- "all of the Three-dimensional Rotation items and more than half of all 60 items were answered incorrectly more often than correctly and the weighted mean for all items was only 0.53."
- Footnote: "the remaining ICAR items can be accessed through ICAR-Project.org. A sample data set based on the items listed in Appendix A is also available ('iqitems') through the psych package".

## What it does not say

- Normative IQ-scale conversion tables: the paper validates against self-reported SAT/ACT and other measures; it does not publish an IQ norm table.
- Any processing-speed item type.

## Checked here (arithmetic, not a quote)

Spearman–Brown from ICAR16 (α 0.81) to 60 items (k = 3.75): 3.75·0.81 / (1 + 2.75·0.81) = **0.941**, against the observed ICAR60 α = **0.93**. The formula predicts the real long form within 0.01.
