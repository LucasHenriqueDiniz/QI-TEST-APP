# Raven's Advanced Progressive Matrices — Pearson product page

> Fetched 2026-10-19 · Publisher store page for the APM. Read for how the publisher frames timed vs untimed administration and how it is scored. Read through WebFetch's summariser; the quotes below are what it returned verbatim.

## What it says

- Administration: "Untimed, individual or group: 40-60 minutes"
- Untimed use: to "differentiate between people at the high end of intellectual ability" (harder items, top 20 percent of the population).
- Timed use: to "assess intellectual efficiency."
- Scoring: "Manual Scoring"; the test "produces a single raw score as well as percentile rank" compared to a norm group; "Summary and detailed percentiles".
- Construct: "a non-verbal estimate of abstract reasoning or fluid intelligence."
- Age range: "12:0-16:0, 17:0 years and older".

## What it does not say

- The number of items or the exact timed-administration limit.
- Any per-item time credit: the only score is the raw number correct, converted to a percentile.
