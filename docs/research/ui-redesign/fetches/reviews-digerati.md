## What it says

**Method.** For each star value 1–5, the feed was asked for up to 199 reviews in "most relevant" order. This was done in Portuguese (hl=pt-BR, gl=BR) and English (hl=en, gl=US). This is a sample of the most-relevant written reviews, not every rating. Themes were counted with keyword regexes (pt + en), so the counts are approximate: one review can hit several themes, and words like "wrong" add noise to "doubt".

## What it does not say

- The sample is "most relevant" first and many reviews are from 2019–2021. Recent behaviour (2025–2026 versions) is under-represented.
- Play's full star histogram (the count per star) is not in the feed, so the share of 1★ among all ratings is unknown.
- Keyword counts were not hand-verified review by review.
