**Observed in screenshots (described by the researcher).** The 8 phone screenshots from the BR listing were downloaded and viewed. **The BR listing uses English screenshots**, not localised.

- **Palette and type:** a dark teal/green chalkboard-textured app background with white line-art figures. The marketing frame is a charcoal background with a coloured fabric-textured caption band at the bottom (orange, green, blue, purple, red, rotating per screenshot). Captions are a condensed bold sans in white. Dark theme throughout.
- **Captions (literal):** "Test your intelligence for free", "Solve the Puzzles", "Check your answers", "Einstein reportedly had an IQ above 150. How high is yours?", "Based on the principles of Raven's Progressive Matrices.", "Can you master the most difficult challenges?"
- **Question screen:** classic Raven layout. On top, a **3×3 matrix** in a thin white frame, with the missing cell drawn as an arrow-shaped empty tag. Below, **8 answer options in a 4×2 grid**, each drawn as an arrow-shaped "tag" that points toward the matrix. At the bottom, "«" and "»" square buttons with a **counter "50 / 54" and, below it, a timer "28:57"** in cyan. Some puzzles are full-bleed patterns (a black-and-white spiral, a dot grid) with a tag-shaped hole to fill. The selected option is highlighted (yellow/orange, or pink in review mode).
- **Answer review:** green ☑ and pink ☒ markers above the options. The correct option is filled green and the user's choice pink ("1 / 54" with only a "»" button).
- **Result screen:** a small rainbow brain logo, "Your IQ score is:", a **big gold number "121"**, the line "Excellent! Your fluid intellect is highly above average compared to the general population.", then a **green bell curve** with σ-band percentages (0.13%, 2.14%, 13.59%, 34.13%…) on a 55–145 axis, with a magenta vertical marker at the score. No certificate and no paywall visible.

## What it does not say

- Nothing about where the ads appear. Reviews say ads appear mid-test and before viewing correct answers (see `reviews-digerati.md`).
- No pause/save behaviour is described. Reviews say leaving the app loses progress.
- The listing text says 54 questions while older reviews say 60, so the test length has changed over versions. The current timer length is not stated in the text (the screenshots show values up to 39:13).
