**Observed in screenshots (described by the researcher).** 8 of the 16 BR-listing screenshots were viewed. They are **landscape (16:9) and English**, unchanged for BR.

- **Palette and type:** each frame is one saturated flat colour (electric blue, coral-orange, hot pink, amber) with a subtle large hexagon pattern. Captions are condensed bold all-caps sans in white, mixing light and bold weights ("IMPROVE YOUR **MEMORY SKILLS**"). Inside the app, game screens are dark and atmospheric (a starfield with a space shuttle, misty mountains) or clean white. Polished and premium.
- **Captions (literal):** "YOUR PERSONALIZED BRAIN TRAINER" with category chips (VOCABULARY, WRITING, CONVERSATION, MATH, MEMORY), "GOOGLE PLAY EDITOR'S CHOICE", "EXPRESS YOURSELF CLEARLY", "EXPAND YOUR VOCABULARY", "IMPROVE YOUR MEMORY SKILLS", "SPEAK WITH MORE CONFIDENCE", "TRACK YOUR PROGRESS AND PERFORMANCE", "40+ GAMES TO ENHANCE COGNITIVE SKILLS"
- **Workout screen:** "Recommended" with a vertical checklist timeline of 5 game cards (Brevity · WRITING, Recall · SPEAKING, Averages · MATH, Attention · MEMORY, Processing · READING). Each card has a hexagon icon, a gradient/illustrated background and a small coloured skill tag.
- **Game screens:** one word or sentence per screen with large type, e.g. "Is she from London or ~~from~~ Paris?" (tap to delete the redundant word), "sobriquet → nickname / book" (a falling-word choice with a shuttle), an "en" letter-keyboard spelling game with Skip/Submit, and "The ~~morale~~ moral of the story…" with ERROR / VALID buttons.
- **Progress screen:** STREAK "5 days", LEAGUE "Sapphire", TOTAL XP "4,000", then a Performance tab with "EPQ" and per-skill horizontal bars (WRITING 2820 · ADVANCED, SPEAKING 3427, READING 3891 · EXPERT, MATH 2823, MEMORY 2260 · INTERMEDIATE). Bottom nav: Today, Leagues, Games, Notifications, Me.
- **Games library:** "Games" with filter chips (ALL, WRITING, SPEAKING, READING), "GAME OF THE WEEK" and "POPULAR" rows of large illustrated cards.
