**Observed in screenshots (described by the researcher).** The 8 phone screenshots from the BR listing (localised pt-BR) were downloaded and viewed.

- **Palette and type:** loud and game-like. Backgrounds are AI-art style (stormy landscapes, neon "cyber" circuit purples and blues). In-app screens use a soft lavender/peach gradient sky with white question cards. Captions are heavy all-caps sans in white on coloured swoosh bands (orange, blue, indigo, green, purple, teal, red, pink). Light-ish UI on a busy frame.
- **Captions (literal, pt-BR):** "TESTE DE QI GRÁTIS · Faça um teste completo em português", "TODAS AS RESPOSTAS EXPLICADAS · Entenda a lógica de cada questão", "DESCUBRA SEU RESULTADO DE QI · Veja sua pontuação ao concluir o teste", "TESTE COMPLETO DE QI · 40 questões de lógica e raciocínio", "APRENDA COM CADA QUESTÃO · Revise seus erros e veja o raciocínio correto", "TREINE PARA PROVAS DE LÓGICA · Concursos, seleções e testes psicotécnicos", "JOGOS DE RACIOCÍNIO LÓGICO · Resolva desafios e avance de nível", "TREINO CEREBRAL TODOS OS DIAS · Lógica, memória, cálculo e atenção"
- **Question screen:** top bar with a pause button (Ⅱ) on the left and a **countdown "29:29"** with a clock icon on the right. Below it, a centred **"8/40"** counter flanked by ← → arrow buttons (free navigation). A white card holds the stimulus: a 3×3 figure matrix with "?", or a 2×2 of colour hexagon flowers, or an isometric cube shape. Answers are a **3×2 grid of white tiles**, and the selected tile turns light blue. Another variant shows ← and ✕ round buttons with an "i" info button, and the correct answer highlighted in green.
- **Explanation screen:** after answering, a text panel explains the rule, e.g. "Por colunas, a figura inferior é obtida adicionando as duas figuras superiores". A "Por quê?" pill and a "Próximo" button appear, with right/wrong tiles marked green and red.
- **Result screen:** "RESULTADO DO TESTE". A **circular gauge (purple-blue arc)** with "QI ESTIMADO" and a **big "128"**, a purple pill "ACIMA DA MÉDIA", a **rainbow horizontal bar 70→130** with a marker, a check row "Excelente raciocínio lógico", and two stat tiles: "40 QUESTÕES" and a stopwatch "29:18". No bell curve, no certificate, no paywall visible.
- **Mini-games:** "Nível 59" number spiral with a numeric keypad and "Verificar". A memory game screenshot shows **Spanish text in the pt-BR set** ("Memoriza la imagen", "Puntuación", "Objetivo"), which is a localisation leak.

## What it does not say

- Coin prices and IAP prices are not on the web listing. One BR review mentions "precisam pagar 25 Reais" (see `reviews-trasco.md`).
- It does not say how many ads appear or when. Reviews say ads appear "a cada jogo", interstitials on pause/resume, a long cookie/vendor consent screen, and rewarded videos needed for explanations.
- It does not say whether a coin is needed for the *first* test. One US review says "First time I started the game immediately says I dont have coins to take the IQ test and need to watch ads" (Dec 2023).
