# Google-served ads on screens without publisher-content - Publisher Policies Help

> Fetched 2026-10-19 · Detail page for the inventory-value rule; read to decide whether a banner may appear on a "calculating your result…" transition screen, a loading screen or an exit screen.

## What it says

> "We do not allow Google-served ads on screens:
> - without publisher-content or with low-value content,
> - that are under construction,
> - that are used for alerts, navigation or other behavioral purposes"

### Tips for understanding this policy

> "The content you provide should be of value to the user and be the focal point for users visiting your site or app. For this reason, we may limit or disable ad serving on pages with low value content and/or still being constructed until changes are made."

> "Additional examples that can help you understand inventory value policy:
> - Ads should not be placed on "dead end" or no content screens (e.g., Thank You, Exit, Error pages, etc.)
> - Ads should not be placed within apps where the sole focus of the interaction is the user looking away from the screen. For example, a flashlight app.
> - Don't place ads on automatically generated content without manual review or curation."

Two example images ("Example 1", "Example 2") have no alt text.

## What it does not say

- Loading/"please wait" screens are not named explicitly. They are screens without publisher content used for a behavioral purpose, so by the stated rule a banner should not be shown on a pure "calculating…" interstitial-lead-in screen; the page does not confirm this case directly.
- Does not address error/offline screens inside a flow other than listing "Error pages" as an example.
