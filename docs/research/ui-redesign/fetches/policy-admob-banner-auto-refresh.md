# Use automatic refresh for your banner ads - Google AdMob Help

> Fetched 2026-10-19 · AdMob refresh setting; scanned for the refresh-rate range relevant to a persistent banner across the test.

## What it says

> "An ad unit's automatic refresh rate determines how often a new ad request is generated for that ad unit."

> "We recommend using the Google optimized automatic refresh rate. The optimized rate is calculated using AdMob historical data to ensure the ads shown in your ad units are being refreshed at the best rate for banner ads."

> "You also have the option to set a custom refresh rate (30-150 seconds) or to disable the Automatic refresh option for an ad unit."

## What it does not say

- Nothing about placement. Only the lines above were read closely.
