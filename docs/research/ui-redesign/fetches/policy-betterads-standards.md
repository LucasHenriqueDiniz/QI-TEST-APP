# The Initial Better Ads Standards - Coalition for Better Ads

> Fetched 2026-10-19 · The Coalition for Better Ads standards index. Not a Google page, but Google Publisher Policies incorporate it by reference ("must not place Google-served ads on screens that do not conform to the Better Ads Standards") and Google Play's "Better Ads Experiences" says it is "inspired by" its Mobile Apps guidelines. Raw HTML could not be downloaded (curl timed out); text below was read through the WebFetch tool, which returned the item names only.

## What it says

Mobile App experiences listed (names as shown):

> "Interruptive interstitials"; "Interruptive video interstitials"; "Non-skippable video interruptive interstitials"; "Video interstitials when opening an app"

Mobile Web experiences listed (for contrast; these apply to mobile web, not apps):

> "Pop-up Ads"; "Prestitial Ads"; "Ad Density Higher Than 30%"; "Flashing Animated Ads"; "Auto-playing Video Ads with Sound"; "Postitial Ads with Countdown"; "Full-screen Scrollover Ads"; "Large Sticky Ads"; "Sticky, Pop-Out Video Ads"; "Sticky Video Ad with Large Inline Ad"

## What it does not say

- The index page shows names, thumbnails and links only; definitions are on the per-experience pages (policy-betterads-app-*.md).
- The mobile-app list contains no banner experience; "Large Sticky Ads" and "Ad Density Higher Than 30%" are mobile-web standards, so they do not directly bind an in-app anchored banner.
