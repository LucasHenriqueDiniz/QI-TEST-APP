# You're on fire! Or, how we brought the streak milestone to life (Duolingo Blog, Kurt Hartfelder, 2022-01-21)

> Fetched 2026-10-19 · Duolingo's own write-up of redesigning its milestone celebration (animation + share card); read as a vendor case for a celebratory "hero" result moment and an in-app share card.

## What it says

> "One of the most rewarding moments in a learner's streak is a "milestone" day—hitting a notable number of consecutive days learning. These are exciting moments, but did they feel exciting enough to the learner?"

> "Though many learners love their streak, our research and focus group studies revealed that some learners new to Duolingo don't understand the streak when beginning their language journey. Why? One hypothesis: the analogy of "keeping the flame alive" or being "on fire" when you've got a rally going is not shared by all cultures across the globe."

> "Then, we had a different idea: what if we treated milestones like "power ups" in a video game, and Duo himself physically changed on these exciting days?"

> "The previous version had Duo holding number balloons. Cute, but not quite celebratory enough, energy-wise."

> "Timing is everything in animation. Multiple passes of rough animation are done to experiment with different variations on an idea before refining the overall rhythm and energy."

> "Last but not least, we wanted learners to be able to share their excitement with everyone! Our talented product designers and engineers came up with a wonderfully simple and attractive share card that allows you to easily save, post, or #humblebrag your achievements with friends, family, and social media without having to leave the Duolingo app."

> "Here at Duolingo we test everything, but we also recognize that there are certain wins that are hard to quantitatively measure. This streak milestone update has proven to be a success on both fronts. Early metrics have indicated that on both iOS and Android devices, more people are keeping their streaks alive, celebrating their milestones, and continue to march towards their language learning goals!"

Context from a second Duolingo post read in the same session ("Improving the streak", Anton Yu, 2020-11-19, https://blog.duolingo.com/improving-the-streak/):

> "our data shows that learners who reach a streak of 7 are 2.4 times more likely to continue using Duolingo the next day than learners without a streak."

> "separating the two mechanics led to a 3.3% increase in Day 14 retention [...] a 1% increase in our overall daily active learners; and, in just 20 days, a 10.5% increase in the percentage of daily learners on a streak. (Note: all results are reported in terms of relative changes.)"

> "Not everything was positive — fewer learners were actually reaching their daily goals without the motivation of the streak"

## What it does not say

- No numbers for the milestone redesign itself ("early metrics" only), and no share-rate numbers for the share card.
- Streaks are a daily-habit mechanic; a one-shot IQ test with optional retakes has no daily loop, so the retention figures above do not transfer. What transfers is the pattern: celebrate the milestone moment with a dedicated animation and attach an in-app share card.
- No description of the share card's format/dimensions or of haptics/sound used in the celebration.
