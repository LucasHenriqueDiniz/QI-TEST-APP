**Observed in screenshots (described by the researcher).** All 5 phone screenshots were viewed. They are the same on BR and US, English only, and are **iPhone captures** (Dynamic Island and iOS status bar).

- **Palette and type:** very dark green-grey (slate-teal) frame and a mint/teal accent. The app UI is plain iOS-style white with light-grey rounded option rows and a solid deep-teal primary button. Flat "undraw-style" teal illustrations. Bold neo-grotesque sans for captions. Light UI.
- **Captions (literal):** "Scientific Tests … and More", "Test Your IQ", "Personalized Recommendations", "Know Yourself", "Reliable Results"
- **Test catalogue:** a 2-column grid of illustrated tiles: IQ Test, Empathy Test, Personality Test, Jealousy Test, Career Aptitude, Multiple Intelligence, Narcissism & Ego, Partner Test. The home "IQLab" screen has a "PRO" gradient badge and "Categories" cards (Cognitive Competence & Intelligence; Personality Traits & Psychological Mechanisms; Emotional & Social Well-Being; Relationship & Career Orientation), with a 2-tab bar (Tests / History).
- **Question screen ("Mini IQ Test"):** a thin progress bar with "0/30 answered" and "Question 1/30", the text prompt "Which number should come next in the following sequence? 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, ___", and **4 full-width stacked option rows** (13, 14, 15, 17) — a list, not a grid. A "Swipe up for next question" hint with a chevron, and a "Finish Test" teal button pinned to the bottom. No timer visible.
- **Result screen (personality):** "Test Results · Personality Dimensions Test" with an illustration, followed by long-form paragraphs per dimension ("Artistic and Cultural Engagement", "Openness and Creativity") that cite the user's own answers ("(Q1)", "(Q2)"). No IQ result screen is shown.
