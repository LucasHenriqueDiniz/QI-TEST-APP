## What it does not say

- No IAP prices. US reviews say a Google Pay purchase prompt appears when the questions end, and that "view answers" leads to a web page asking for card details and a 39.99/40 USD charge (see `reviews-onlinetools.md`). That contradicts "Saber o resultado do teste é gratuito". Other reviews say the result was free, so behaviour may differ by version or flow.
- The claim "muito preciso" (very accurate) has no supporting evidence.
- Its BR reviews complain that the app is not in Portuguese, although the listing description is.
