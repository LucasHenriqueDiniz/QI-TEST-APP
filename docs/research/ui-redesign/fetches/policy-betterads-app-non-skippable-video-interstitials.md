# Ad Experience: Non-skippable video interruptive interstitials - Mobile Apps (Coalition for Better Ads)

> Fetched 2026-10-19 · Better Ads definition behind Google Play's 15-second closeability rule. Raw HTML could not be downloaded (curl timed out); quotes are fragments returned by the WebFetch tool.

## What it says

> "A non-skippable interruptive video interstitial ad is a full screen video ad" … "that appears at an unexpected point in game play or the content segment" … "when users have chosen to do something else or are already engaged in doing something else" … "and cannot be skipped or dismissed."

Tested experiences listed: "15s Video Interstitial Pre-Game / Pre-Content No Skip"; "15s Video Interstitial During Game Play / Content Segment No Skip"; "15s Video Interstitial At End Of Game Play / Content Segment No Skip"; "Video Interstitial At End of Game Play / Content Segment No Skip".

## What it does not say

- The tool did not return which of the tested experiences fell beneath the standard, so this page alone does not tell whether a non-skippable ad *at the end* of a content segment is acceptable. Google Play's text answers it for Play: such interstitials "that do not interrupt users in their actions (for example, after the score screen in a game app) may persist more than 15 seconds" (policy-play-ads.md).
