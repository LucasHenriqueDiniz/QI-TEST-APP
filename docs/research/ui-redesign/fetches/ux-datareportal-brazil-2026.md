# Digital 2026: Brazil (DataReportal / Kepios)

> Fetched 2026-10-19 · Annual digital-usage report for Brazil (aggregates GSMA Intelligence, Meta and Google ad-tool data); read to size Instagram (and look for WhatsApp) as share targets for a story-format result card.

## What it says

> "At the time of report production, Kepios's analysis of the latest available data indicated that there were 185 million internet users in Brazil in October 2025."

> "Brazil was home to 150 million social media user identities in October 2025, equating to 70.4 percent of the total population."

> "Numbers published in Meta's advertising tools indicate that Instagram had 147 million users in Brazil in late 2025."

> "Meanwhile, data published in the company's ad tools indicate that 87.3 percent of adults aged 18 and above in Brazil used Instagram at the end of 2025."

> "Similarly, it may be helpful to know that Instagram's ad reach in Brazil at the end of 2025 was equivalent to 79.5 percent of the local internet user base (regardless of age)."

> "Google's advertising resources indicate that YouTube had 150 million users in Brazil in late 2025."

> "[...] 97.4 percent of mobile connections in Brazil can now be considered "broadband", which means that they connect via 3G, 4G, or 5G mobile networks."

Caveat stated on the page:

> "It's important to stress that these advertising reach figures do not necessarily represent the same thing as monthly active user figures though"

## What it does not say

- The page text I extracted has no WhatsApp figure (WhatsApp has no ad-reach data in Meta's tools). A WhatsApp penetration number for Brazil (third-party summaries cite ~93.7% of internet users, attributed to an earlier DataReportal edition via Statista) is **not** verified here.
- Nothing on how often Brazilians share images to WhatsApp Status vs Instagram Stories, or on story-format sharing behaviour.
- No Android vs iOS split on this page as extracted.
