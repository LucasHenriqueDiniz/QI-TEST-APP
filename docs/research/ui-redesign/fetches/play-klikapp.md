## What it does not say

- Prices of the "Premium" and other IAPs are not shown. One review says "in order for you to get the results it's $3.49" (Apr 2026), but a 5★ review from Aug 2026 says "you get your marks for free". It is unresolved whether the score itself is paywalled in the current version (see `reviews-klikapp.md`).
- The listing does not disclose that the certificate needs ads (one review says "3 adds") or that answer review appears to be Premium (crown badge). Both come from screenshots and reviews, not the listing text.
- The certificate names "Lumensa IQ test", which is also the title of another Klikapp listing ("Lumensa IQ Test & Puzzles", seen in `play-search-qi.md`). The listing does not explain this.
