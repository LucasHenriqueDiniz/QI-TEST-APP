# AdMob Banner Ad Implementation Guidance (Google blog, 2015)

> Fetched 2026-10-19 · Google's AdMob blog post (John Brown, Policy Transparency Lead, September 22, 2015) announcing the banner guidance; read in the hope that it gave a numeric spacing. Read through the WebFetch tool (short fragments only).

## What it says

> "Close proximity of banner ads to other elements within an app is one of the biggest causes of accidental clicks."

> "banner ads should not be placed next to interactive navigational buttons"

> "separating the banner ad from app interactive elements helps reduce user confusion"

## What it does not say

- No numeric distance (px/dp), no border specification, no top/bottom rule. It refers readers to the AdMob Help Center for visual examples.
