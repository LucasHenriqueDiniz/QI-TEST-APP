# Banner ad guidance: 300x250 - Google AdMob Help

> Fetched 2026-10-19 · AdMob guidance for the medium-rectangle banner; read because a redesign might put an inline 300x250 on the results screen, and for its "screens with no content" and "level complete screen" rules.

## What it says

> "While all AdMob/AdSense policies and the information on banner ad guidance still apply, we want to share specific guidance regarding this ad size due to its unique size and shape."

### Ad mimicking app content (against policy)

> "Ads should not mimic app content. They must be clearly distinguishable from the app content. For example, in galleries, ads must be clearly delineated that they are not a feature image in the gallery themselves."

### Ad overlapping with app content (against policy)

> "Ads should not float or hover over app content. This includes placing the ad over a background that covers app content. Ads should not unexpectedly appear and cover app content."

### Ad on screens with no content (against policy)

> "Ads should not be placed on a 'dead end' screen. Additionally, there must be a way to exit a screen without clicking the ad (for example, a 'back' or 'menu' button). Otherwise, the user should be notified that the home button will exit the app."

### Ad adjacent to interactive elements

> "Close proximity of ads to other elements within an app is one of the biggest causes of accidental clicks. To avoid accidental clicks, banner ads should not be placed immediately next to interactive elements and app content, which can include (but is not limited to):
> - Navigational buttons, such as a "next" button.
> - Interactive content like a text chat box or an image in an image gallery.
> - On a game play screen where users are continuously interacting with the app.
> - A "close" button or something equivalent on an app exit confirmation screen."

### Custom close or dismiss methods

> "Custom close or dismiss methods include but is not limited to, a custom X button, a custom close button, or text that indicates the user should click it to close the ad. These buttons or text should not overlap or be adjacent to the ad since those placements may cause a high volume of accidental clicks."

### Pause or level complete screen

> "Placing a 300x250 ad on a pause or level complete screen may be compliant as long as you take care that users are not encouraged to or tricked into clicking on the ad to resume the app. The ad should also be distinguishable from other interactive elements on the the screen."

### Clear representation of ad

> "Ads should be distinguishable from app content. It is recommended that you label, position, or represent ads in a way that lets users know that it's a sponsored ad and is not part of the app content. However, don't label ads with any messaging that encourages users to click on or view ads."

## What it does not say

- No spacing value.
- The "level complete screen" allowance is the nearest analogue to an IQ results screen, but the page does not discuss results screens with share/retry buttons specifically.
